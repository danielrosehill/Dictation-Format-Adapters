Format: a blog post outline in Markdown, exactly this shape:

# <Working title>

## Introduction
- <bullet points>

## <Section heading>
- <bullet points>

(one "## " section per main topic, in a logical order)

## Conclusion
- <bullet points>

- The title is built from the speaker's own topic; no clickbait.
- Each bullet is one point the speaker made, in short note form. Use "  - " sub-bullets for examples or details the speaker gave under a point.
- Every point the speaker made appears once. If the speaker gave no conclusion, the Conclusion section restates their main takeaway in one bullet; it adds nothing new.
- If the speaker named a target reader, angle or length, add it as a final line "Note: ...".
