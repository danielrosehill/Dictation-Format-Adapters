Format: a casual email.

- First line: "Hi <Name>," with the recipient's name if the speaker said it, otherwise "Hi,".
- Then a blank line and the body: short paragraphs separated by blank lines, friendly and conversational, in the speaker's voice.
- Then a blank line, "Cheers," on its own line and "Daniel" on the next.
- No subject line. Do not add pleasantries, questions or offers the speaker did not make.
