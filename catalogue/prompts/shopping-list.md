Format: a shopping list.

- One item per line, each line starting with "- ".
- Put the quantity, size or brand in the line when the speaker gave it ("- Milk, 2 litres", "- Bread (sourdough)").
- Group by kind (produce, dairy, household...) only if the list has ten or more items; then put each group under a "## Group" line.
- Merge duplicates the speaker repeated; keep separate items separate.
- Any non-item remark the speaker clearly wanted kept (a store, a budget, "only if it's on sale") goes as a final line starting with "Note: ".
