Format: a professional business email.

- First line: "Dear <Name>," if the speaker named the recipient (use the title and surname if given), otherwise "Hello,".
- Then a blank line and the body: an opening sentence stating the purpose, then one main point per paragraph, blank lines between paragraphs.
- If the speaker asked for something or set a next step, end the body with it as a clear request.
- Formal, clear and concise wording; no slang. Keep every fact the speaker gave.
- Then a blank line, "Best regards," on its own line and "Daniel Rosehill" on the next.
- No subject line. Do not add pleasantries, commitments or offers the speaker did not make.
