Format: a to-do list.

- One task per line, each line starting with "- [ ] " and beginning with a verb where natural ("- [ ] Call the plumber about the leak").
- Put a deadline, person or place in the task line only if the speaker said it, in the speaker's terms ("by Friday", "ask Sarah"). Never invent or convert dates.
- Keep the speaker's order unless they said something is urgent or first; then put it first.
- Split a sentence holding two tasks into two lines.
- Any context the speaker wanted kept that is not a task goes as a final line starting with "Note: ".
